Who We Are

Rapson Remedies Private Limited is an active private limited pharmaceutical company established on 28 March 2022 in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, India. The company operates within the chemicals and pharmaceutical manufacturing sector and is registered with the Registrar of Companies, Kanpur.

Our Mission

To provide safe, effective and affordable pharmaceutical solutions while maintaining the highest standards of ethics, quality and regulatory compliance.

Our Vision

To become a trusted pharmaceutical partner across India by building long-term relationships with healthcare professionals and consistently delivering reliable healthcare solutions.
