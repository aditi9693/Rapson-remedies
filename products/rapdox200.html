Hard Hitting Antibacterial

RAPDOX-200

Antibiotic With Striking Efficacy.

Composition
Cefpodoxime Proxetil — 200mg

An oral third-generation cephalosporin that acts as a prodrug, converted in the body to its active form, Cefpodoxime, to fight bacterial cell wall synthesis.

What it does

RAPDOX-200 provides broad-spectrum antibacterial activity against both Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria. It is highly stable in the presence of beta-lactamases, enzymes produced by bacteria to resist standard antibiotics. By inhibiting the final transpeptidation step of peptidoglycan synthesis, it effectively destroys the bacterial cell wall.

Why it's better
Superior Safety Profile

Clinically proven to have a significantly better safety profile and patient compliance compared to many standard antibiotic combinations.

Empirical Therapy Choice

Highly stable against beta-lactamases, making it a reliable choice for empirical therapy before specific culture results are available.

Striking Efficacy

A 5-day course is clinically effective as an 8-day treatment of other major antibiotics for Pharyngotonsillitis.

Clinical Indications

Ceforap is highly effective in treating a broad spectrum of bacterial infections:

  • Recurrent Acute Pharyngotonsillitis
  • Pediatric Typhoid Fever
  • Uncomplicated Urinary Tract Infections (UTI)
  • ENT Infections (Ear, Nose, Throat)
  • Skin and Soft Tissue Infections
  • Respiratory Tract Infections
  • Acute & Chronic Sinusitis
  • Anti-Microbial Prophylaxis in Surgical Procedures
