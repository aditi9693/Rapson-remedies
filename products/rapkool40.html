Essential Gastroenterology

Rapkool 40

The trusted gold standard for gastric acid suppression.

Detailed Ingredients
Pantoprazole Sodium (40mg)

Each enteric-coated tablet contains Pantoprazole Sodium equivalent to Pantoprazole 40mg. The enteric coating ensures the medication bypasses stomach acid to be absorbed effectively in the intestine.

What it Does

Rapkool 40 is a Proton Pump Inhibitor (PPI). It works by binding to the H+/K+ ATPase enzyme system (the "acid pump") in the stomach lining. This action shuts down acid production at the source, allowing the esophagus and stomach lining time to heal.

Why it's Better
High Bioavailability

Provides rapid and sustained relief from heartburn and acid regurgitation within the first few doses.

Superior Safety Profile

Pantoprazole is clinically preferred for long-term use due to its minimal interaction with other common medications.

Clinical Indications
  • GERD: Treatment of Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease and associated heartburn.
  • Peptic Ulcers: Healing and maintenance of Duodenal and Benign Gastric Ulcers.
  • Erosive Esophagitis: Treatment of inflammation and sores in the food pipe.
  • Zollinger-Ellison Syndrome: Management of pathological hypersecretory conditions.
  • NSAID Protection: Prevention of ulcers in patients taking long-term pain medication.
