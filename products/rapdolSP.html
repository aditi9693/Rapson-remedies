Pain Management

Rapdol-SP Tab.

The right choice to relieve pain & inflammation.

Detailed Ingredients
Aceclofenac (100mg)

A non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID) that reduces pain and prevents further inflammation.

Paracetamol (325mg)

An analgesic and antipyretic agent that provides highly antipyretic effects.

Serratiopeptidase (15mg)

A proteolytic enzyme with fibrinolytic action that clears tissue debris and improves micro-circulation to hasten recovery.

What it Does

Rapdol-SP acts as a triple-threat against physical trauma. While Aceclofenac and Paracetamol work synergistically to block pain signals and reduce swelling, Serratiopeptidase actively breaks down abnormal proteins at the site of injury to improve blood flow and accelerate the natural healing process.

Why it's Better
Fibrinolytic Action

Serratiopeptidase clears debris that other standard painkillers leave behind.

Accelerated Recovery

Hastens recovery by improving micro-circulation at the inflammatory site.

Clinical Indication
  • Low Back Pain & Dental Pain
  • Spondylitis, Sprains & Strains
  • Traumatic Injury & Hematoma
  • Fracture, Dislocation & Injury related Oedema
