Dermatology / Antibacterial

Crapibact Ointment

Skin infections take us to the darker side of life; bring back the light.

Detailed Ingredients
Mupirocin IP (2% w/w)

A specialized topical antibacterial agent that inhibits bacterial protein synthesis. It is delivered in a smooth ointment base to ensure optimal skin penetration.

Available in convenient 5g & 15g Tubes.

What it Does
  • Provides excellent activity against gram +ve staphylococci and streptococci.
  • Specifically targets S.aureus and S.pyogenes, the primary bacteria responsible for most skin infections.
  • Eliminates bacteria in traumatic skin lesions and primary infections.
Why it's Better
Targeted Topical Action

Direct application to the site of infection ensures high local concentrations without systemic side effects.

Dual Infection Coverage

Highly effective for both primary infections (like impetigo) and secondary infections in traumatic wounds.

Susceptible Strain Eradication

Optimized for rapid clearance of resistant strains of S.aureus and S.pyogenes.

Clinical Indication

Crapibact is clinically indicated for:

  • Furuncles (Boils): Deep-seated bacterial infections of hair follicles.
  • Pyoderma: Any pyogenic (pus-forming) skin infection.
  • Open Wounds: Prevention and treatment of infection in traumatic skin lesions.
  • Impetigo: Highly contagious skin sores, particularly common in children.
