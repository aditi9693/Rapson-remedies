Anti-Allergic

Nollergy-L 60ml Susp.

Allergies make breathing an uphill task. Live free in all seasons.

Detailed Ingredients
Levocetirizine HCl (2.5 mg)

A potent, selective long-acting histamine receptor antagonist designed for maximum efficacy with minimal side effects.

Montelukast Sodium (5 mg)

A powerful selective leukotriene antagonist that inhibits the pro-inflammatory process in the lungs and nasal passages.

What it Does

Nollergy-L works by blocking two different inflammatory pathways. Levocetirizine stops the histamine response to environmental triggers, while Montelukast prevents bronchoconstriction and airway narrowing. This combination ensures smooth airflow and reduces nasal congestion.
