Detailed Ingredients
Cefixime (100mg / 200mg / 400mg)

A third-generation oral cephalosporin with broad-spectrum activity.

What it Does

Cefixime is highly effective in reducing elevated body temperature and high white blood cell counts. It works by inhibiting bacterial cell wall synthesis to eliminate infection-causing pathogens.
