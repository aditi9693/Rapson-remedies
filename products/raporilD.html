Raporil-D

Quiet the cough, restore your comfort.

Detailed Ingredients
Dextromethorphan HBr + CPM + Phenylephrine HCl

A balanced combination of a non-narcotic cough suppressant (Dextromethorphan), an antihistamine (Chlorpheniramine Maleate), and a decongestant (Phenylephrine).

What it Does

Raporil-D acts on the cough center in the brain to suppress the urge to cough, while simultaneously shrinking swollen blood vessels in the nasal passages and blocking histamine receptors to stop allergy-like symptoms.

Why it's Better
Non-Narcotic Relief

Suppresses cough without the risk of addiction or heavy sedation associated with codeine-based syrups.

Multi-Symptom Action

One dose manages cough, cold, and nasal congestion simultaneously.

Clinical Indications
  • Relief from dry, irritating, and non-productive cough.
  • Reduces nasal congestion and runny nose (allergic rhinitis).
  • Suppresses the cough reflex triggered by throat irritation.
  • Alleviates sneezing and watery eyes associated with common cold.
