Detailed Ingredients

Relieves pain and inflammation by inhibiting the biosynthesis of prostaglandins.

Methyl Salicylate provides counter-irritant action, while Menthol delivers a cooling sensation to the affected area.

D-Relief Gel acts locally to block the chemical messengers that cause inflammation and pain. Its multi-ingredient formula provides a cooling-then-warming effect that distracts from deep-seated muscle and joint discomfort.
