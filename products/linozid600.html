Antibiotic Therapy

Linozid-600 Tablet

Top in efficacy, Top in Result for serious Gram (+)ve infections.

Detailed Ingredients
Linezolid IP (600mg)

Belongs to a new class of antibiotics known as oxazolidinones.

What it Does

Linezolid acts primarily against Gram-positive bacteria by inhibiting bacterial protein synthesis. This prevents the bacteria from growing and reproducing.

Why it's Better
High Tolerance

Well tolerated with low rates of discontinuation compared to other advanced antibiotics.

MDR-TB Efficacy

May have significant efficacy in the treatment of Multi-Drug Resistant Tuberculosis (MDR-TB).

Clinical Indication
  • Bone & Joint Infections
  • Skin & Soft Tissue Infections
  • Respiratory Tract Infections
  • Serious infections caused by multi-drug resistant Gram-positive bacteria
