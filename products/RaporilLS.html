Mucolytic & Bronchodilator

Raporil-LS

Clear the airways, breathe with ease.

Detailed Ingredients
Levosalbutamol + Ambroxol + Guaifenesin

A synergistic blend of a bronchodilator (Levosalbutamol) to open lungs, a mucolytic (Ambroxol) to thin mucus, and an expectorant (Guaifenesin) to aid expulsion.

What it Does

Raporil-LS relaxes the smooth muscles of the bronchial tubes to improve airflow while chemically breaking down thick phlegm, making it easier to cough out and clear the chest.

Why it's Better
Advanced Bronchodilation

Levosalbutamol provides faster relief with lower heart-rate-related side effects compared to standard Salbutamol.

Triple Clearance

Combines thinning, loosening, and expelling of mucus in one comprehensive formula.

Clinical Indications
  • Facilitates the expulsion of thick, stubborn mucus/phlegm.
  • Relieves chest congestion and respiratory heaviness.
  • Management of productive cough in Bronchitis and Asthma.
  • Opens constricted airways to reduce wheezing and shortness of breath.
