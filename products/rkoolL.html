R Kool-L

The dual-action solution for GERD and Gastric Motility.

Detailed Ingredients
Rabeprazole Sodium (20mg)

An enteric-coated Proton Pump Inhibitor (PPI). It works by reducing the amount of acid produced in the stomach, allowing the esophagus to heal and preventing chronic heartburn and acid reflux.

Levosulpiride (75mg)

A sustained-release prokinetic agent. It increases the movement of the stomach and intestines, ensuring that food moves through the digestive tract faster, effectively reducing bloating and the feeling of fullness.

What it does

R Kool-L addresses the root cause of Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD). While Rabeprazole handles the chemical aspect (acid suppression), Levosulpiride handles the mechanical aspect (gut motility). Together, they stop acid from rising and ensure the stomach empties efficiently, providing 24-hour relief.

Why it's better
Superior PPI Action

Rabeprazole has a faster onset of action compared to older PPIs like Omeprazole, meaning the patient feels relief sooner.

SR Technology

The Levosulpiride is in Sustained Release form, maintaining a consistent level of the drug in the bloodstream for all-day comfort.

Dual Path Therapy

Unlike simple antacids, R Kool-L treats both the symptoms and the mechanical stomach disorders simultaneously.

Excellent Safety Profile

Levosulpiride is known for having fewer side effects on the central nervous system compared to other prokinetics like Metoclopramide.

Clinical Indications
  • Hyperacidity & Heartburn
  • Dyspepsia (Indigestion) with Bloating
  • Diabetic Gastroparesis
  • Post-operative Gastric Emptying issues
