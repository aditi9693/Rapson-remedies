Rapilast-FX Tab.

Take prevention before allergy worsens. Easy to breath, Easy to relax.

Detailed Ingredients
Fexofenadine Hydrochloride (120mg)

A second-generation, non-sedative antihistamine that targets H1 receptors to block allergic reactions without affecting alertness.

Montelukast Sodium (10mg)

A powerful leukotriene receptor antagonist that reduces inflammation in the airways and prevents asthma symptoms.

What it Does

Rapilast-FX provides a dual-action mechanism. Fexofenadine reduces allergic inflammation caused by mast cells, basophils, and eosinophils. Simultaneously, Montelukast blocks leukotrienes to prevent bronchoconstriction, making it easier to breathe during allergic flares.

Why it's Better
Truly Non-Sedative

Unlike older antihistamines, Fexofenadine does not cross the blood-brain barrier, ensuring zero drowsiness.

FDA Approved

Montelukast is FDA approved specifically for the management of seasonal allergic rhinitis.

24-Hour Coverage

A single tablet provides full-day protection against environmental allergens and exercise-induced asthma.

Clinical Indication
  • Allergic Rhinitis: Relief from sneezing, runny nose, and itchy eyes.
  • Asthma: Prophylactic treatment for chronic asthma management.
  • COPD: Supportive therapy for chronic obstructive pulmonary disease symptoms.
  • Smoker's Cough: Alleviates airway sensitivity associated with chronic smoking.
